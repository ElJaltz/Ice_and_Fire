Within these pages are collected notes on dragons and other mythical creatures.
Scholars of the far north claim that dragons once ruled the skies, leaving ruins and treasure in their wake.
Adventurers can assemble this Bestiary by combining Manuscripts at a Lectern.

The first step is to gather knowledge and resources.
Sapphires and other rare minerals are prized by researchers and craftsmen alike.
Manuscripts found in dungeons and lairs can be bound into this book to unlock new chapters.

Use this tome to learn the fundamentals of taming, feeding and commanding great beasts.
Future pages will teach you about dragon bones, scales, alchemy and the tools required to master them.
Stay vigilant: riches and danger often come hand in hand.
